About Me

I am a Biomedical Engineer specializing in biomedical imaging, deep learning, and biosignal analysis. My research explores how artificial intelligence can improve disease diagnosis and healthcare accessibility, particularly in resource-limited settings. I hold an MSc in Biomedical Engineering (Biomedical Imaging) from Jimma University, Ethiopia.

Research Interests

  • Medical image analysis using deep learning (fundus, ultrasound,x-ray/CTscan)
  • AI-assisted disease classification and detection
  • Biomedical signal processing (EEG,EMG, FTIR spectroscopy)
  • Low-cost medical device innovation and instrumentation
